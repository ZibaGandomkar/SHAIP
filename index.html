Human-centred AI · Medical imaging · Perception

We shape how AI transforms medical imaging.

At SHAIP Lab, we study the full relationship between artificial intelligence and medical imaging — from building AI and curating the data behind it to understanding how radiologists see, decide and learn with AI.

Our centre of gravity is human–AI interaction: making sure advances in algorithms, imaging and education translate into better decisions, better expertise and better care.

Medical imaging AI is not simply an algorithmic problem. It changes how images are created, how clinicians perceive and act on information, how expertise develops, how risk is communicated, and who benefits from innovation.

Across everything we do

Three principles shape the lab.

Equity & responsible AI

We examine calibration, fairness, generalisability and whether AI delivers benefit across diverse populations and settings.

Personalisation

We explore AI that adapts to the patient, the clinician, the learner and the clinical context.

Translation

We connect algorithms to imaging workflows, human behaviour, education and real-world implementation.

The future of medical imaging will be shaped by the interaction between people, AI, data and imaging technology. SHAIP exists to study that interaction as one system.
