Education

Teaching for the era of AI.

SHAIP Lab investigates both sides of the education challenge: how imaging professionals learn to work safely and effectively with AI, and how AI can be designed to improve learning itself.

Research → Education → Practice

AI changes the expertise imaging professionals need — and gives us new tools to develop that expertise.

Two directions, one program.

Learning to work with AI

We investigate how practitioners should develop AI literacy, calibrate trust, recognise failure modes and maintain perceptual expertise in AI-enabled practice.

Using AI to improve learning

We explore personalised learning, adaptive case selection, intelligent feedback, generative AI and AI-supported curriculum design.

Generative AI as a learning scaffold

We study how GenAI can support questioning, explanation, feedback, reflection and verification without replacing the cognitive work that develops clinical reasoning. Our focus is not simply whether learners use AI, but how the learning design around AI changes outcomes.

Teaching across the medical imaging curriculum

SHAIP's educational work includes dedicated teaching in AI in Medical Imaging, as well as AI components embedded within imaging informatics, data management, RIS/PACS and clinical workflow education.

Why this matters

Education is not an afterthought to implementation. If AI changes the tasks clinicians perform, the information they see and the errors they are likely to make, education needs to evolve at the same time.

We want education to be part of AI design.

Our goal is to use evidence from perception, expertise and human–AI interaction to inform both workforce preparation and the next generation of intelligent learning tools.
