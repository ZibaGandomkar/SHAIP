Research

Four connected research programs.

SHAIP Lab brings technical AI development together with the science of how people perceive, use and learn with AI. Our programs are distinct, but deliberately designed to intersect.

01

AI for Risk, Diagnosis & Characterisation

We develop and evaluate AI that extracts clinically meaningful information from medical images: predicting risk, supporting diagnosis, characterising disease and identifying opportunities for earlier intervention.

Stream lead: Ziba Gandomkar
Breast imagingRisk predictionRadiomicsHistopathologyUltrasoundOpportunistic screeningCalibration & equity
02

Human–AI Interaction & Perception

We investigate how AI changes visual search, interpretation, confidence, reliance and decision-making. This includes when AI should assist, how assistance should be personalised, and how AI-derived information should be communicated to clinicians and consumers.

Stream lead: Nicola Giannotti
Reader studiesCase difficultyHuman–AI pairingAdaptive assistanceEye trackingRisk communication
03

AI-Augmented Imaging Innovation

We use AI to improve how images are acquired, reconstructed, enhanced and quantified. The goal is to make imaging itself more informative and efficient, while evaluating whether these technical improvements translate into better clinical use.

Stream lead: Seyedamir Tavakoli Taba
Phase-contrast imagingAI denoisingReconstructionSegmentationBreast MRIShoulder MRIBAC quantification
04

AI, Education & Expertise Development

We study how expertise should develop in an AI-rich imaging environment and how AI itself can improve education through personalisation, adaptive case selection, intelligent feedback and curriculum support.

Stream lead: Dania Abu Awwad
AI literacyExpertise developmentPersonalised learningGenerative AIAdaptive educationWorkforce readiness

Foundation: Imaging Data & Evaluation Infrastructure

We curate imaging, clinical, reader and outcome datasets and develop evaluation frameworks that connect model performance to human performance, workflow and clinical impact.

Foundation lead: Ernest Ekpo
